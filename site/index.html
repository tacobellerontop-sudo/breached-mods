Breached mods

Breached leaves out the big features most browsers come with, so it stays light. Mods bring them back, one at a time, for the people who want them.

Press Install on a mod and Breached asks you to confirm. Installed mods show in Settings > Mods, where you can change or remove them.

Open this page in Breached to install mods with one click. Or download a mod's zip here and add it in Breached's Settings > Mods > Add a mod from a .zip.

Mods run with Breached's full access, like the browser itself. The ones here are made by the Breached project and their code is on GitHub.
